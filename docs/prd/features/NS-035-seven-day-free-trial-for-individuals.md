# NS-035: Seven-day free trial for individuals

[Back to the PRD](../README.md)

**Requirement ID:** NS-035

**User Story:** As an individual user, I want to try the product free for 7 days so that I can decide before paying.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- [ ] New individual accounts can start a 7-day trial; teams and enterprise workspaces cannot.
- [ ] The trial includes up to 30 minutes of stored video and a capped amount of AI usage (proposed: $10 of tokens).
- [ ] When a cap is reached, recording or AI features pause with a message offering an upgrade.
- [ ] Users see days remaining, usage against caps and the trial end date.
- [ ] The trial converts to a paid subscription at its end only if a payment method was added; otherwise access becomes read-only.
- [ ] Each person can use the trial once.

**Technical Notes:** Whether a card is required up front and the final AI cap are open questions. Abuse prevention by email and payment fingerprint.

**Priority:** Must-have
