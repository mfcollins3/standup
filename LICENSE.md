## FAQ

### 1. What is a source-available license?

A source-available license allows me to openly share the source code for
software products that I am developing with others, while retaining my
commercial rights to the product. I am granting you access to review my source
code and make derivative works of my product for your own personal use or your
company's internal use. In the future, if I commercialize the product, you can
continue to use the source code and your derivative works, but you will be
required to purchase a commercial license to do so.

### 2. Will the Software remain source-available after commercialization?

Yes. If I commercialize the Software in the future and begin charging a
licensing fee, the source code for the Software will remain source-available.
You will continue to have access to the latest source code and may use any
derivative versions you have created.

### 3. Why are you licensing the Software like this?

While I contribute to open source and have released products I have created as
open source, I am a professional software developer with goals and a family,
and I would like to be compensated for the use of certain works I create that
have potential commercial value. Once the product reaches a point where it can
be commercialized, it is in everyone's best interest that I retain this right.
A commercial license brings with it the expectation that I will continue to
invest in the product by adding features, fixing bugs, evolving the product,
and providing customer support.

By developing the Software under a source-available license, I am able to share
the product and develop it openly. I do not have to work on it in the shadows.
If my work is good enough, early adopters can begin to play with the product and
provide me with feedback during the early parts of development to make the
Software better.

### 4. If I create an extension or integration for your product, can I charge for its use?

Yes. Under the license terms, any extensions or integrations that use anything
deemed a Public API or package (code under the `/pkg` directory or REST, gRPC,
or MCP APIs), directly access and use the user's data in a database or other
data store, or invoke CLI commands exposed by the Software's CLI interface are
your products. You may charge for them if you wish.

Extensions and integrations should be original works. If you directly copy and
reuse code from my product in your extension, then you are violating my
copyright, and the extension would not be considered an original work.

If you need code that I have written in my product to make your extension or
integration work, reach out and talk to me about it either via email or,
preferably, in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mfcollins3/standup/discussions).
If I can expose that functionality for you to reuse, I'm happy to do it if it
makes sense for the product. If it does not make sense, but I agree that it's
critical for your integration to work, I may grant you written permission to
use my code. But I'm very interested in building an open and extensible
product and supporting a developer community that wants to build on top of my
work. If I can help you to complete your vision, I'm happy to do so. Just keep
in mind that I have to balance the good of the product and all users, and I
have a responsibility to ensure that I continue to make the product usable by
everyone.

### 5. Can I use the source code and make modifications to it?

Yes. You can take my source code and make modifications to it, as long as it is
